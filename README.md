## Key Insights

- Positive sentiment dominates overall communication, with over 70% of messages classified as Positive.
- Certain months show spikes in negativity, often isolated to specific individuals.
- Neutral messages tend to be longer and more detailed, likely reflecting procedural or transactional communication.
- Frequent communicators may be either high-engagement leaders or signs of stress or overload.

## Recommendations

- Monitor at-risk employees proactively to understand causes of sustained negativity.
- Incorporate richer behavioral and text features (e.g., emotion, topic modeling) into future predictive models.
- Use advanced machine learning models (e.g., XGBoost, Random Forest) to improve prediction accuracy.
- Consider integrating this analysis into regular HR reporting dashboards for early intervention.
